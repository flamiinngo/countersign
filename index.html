Countersign

An independent record of when a webhook was sent and when it was actually received, so “we never got that” has an answer neither company has to be trusted for.

ReceiverKey Receiver · long-lived Registered at integration time. Outlives every proof that verifies against it, so a signature can never be disowned after the fact.
SendProof Sender · short, or extended Goes out with the webhook, carrying expectedBy. Anything still alive past its deadline is already the missing set.
ReceiptProof Sender · expires with its pair Holds the receiver's own signed response, captured at delivery. The receiver never has to act during a dispute for the record to exist.
